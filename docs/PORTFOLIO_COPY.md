## LinkedIn draft — review before posting
Late delivery and repeat purchase told different stories in my ecommerce portfolio analysis.

Across delivered Olist orders, late deliveries were associated with average review scores of 2.27 versus 4.29 on time. But a day-30 landmark analysis found an adjusted repeat-purchase difference of about −0.16 percentage points with an interval spanning zero. That is uncertainty, not proof that delivery does not matter.

The practical recommendation is to investigate the delivery experience in MA, CE and BA and test customer outcomes prospectively. I would not book projected retention savings from this association.

The project includes SQL/dbt tests, a live dashboard, source-linked evidence and local-model evaluations that expose routing failures. The most useful AI lesson was that a valid number can still answer the wrong question.
